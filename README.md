# Flight Fare Prediction

Predict the average fare for an airline route from route and market data.

## Approach

- Exploratory analysis of distance, passengers and market share
- Linear Regression, Lasso, ElasticNet, Decision Tree and Random Forest

## Results

Ordinary least squares R-squared of about 0.95 to 0.98 in the saved notebook output.
